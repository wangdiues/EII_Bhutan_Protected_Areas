## Study Overview
**Scope:** 19 protected areas, 19,750 km²  
**Resolution:** 300 m  

**Indicator:** Ecosystem Integrity Index (EII)  
- Functional
- Structural
- Compositional

**Methods:** Zonal statistics, buffer comparisons, regression, machine learning

---

## Key Findings
- Network-wide integrity moderate-to-high
- Functional integrity most variable
- Corridors highly heterogeneous
- Elevation and human modification dominate
