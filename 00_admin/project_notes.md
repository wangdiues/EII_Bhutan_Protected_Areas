# Project Notes — Ecosystem Integrity Index (EII) for Bhutan

## Study Summary
- National assessment of 19 protected areas
- 300-m resolution
- Network mean EII: 0.6785
- Elevation + human modification dominate
- 13 PAs higher inside boundaries
- Corridors highly variable

---

## Data Sources
- Global EII product
- MODIS NDVI and fire
- Hansen forest change
- ESA WorldCover
- SRTM DEM
- Human Modification Index
- National PA boundaries

---

## Methods Highlights
- Zonal statistics
- Category aggregation
- 10-km buffers
- Regression with clustered SEs
- Random-Forest importance
- Proxy-based validation

---

## Key Findings
- Structural & compositional integrity high
- Functional integrity drives contrasts
- Lowland PAs lower
- Corridor 5 and Phibsoo priority for restoration

---

## Limitations
- Modeled index
- Limited field validation
- Single snapshot
- Fixed buffer
- Moderate explanatory power
